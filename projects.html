Johnson & Johnson University Case Competition

Barbed Suture Market Entry

A five-person team strategy for a wound closure leader with no barbed suture product. We made five linked decisions on R&D, pricing, marketing, supply chain, and staffing, then ran the combinations through a 10-year model. The chosen mix projects $23.2B NPV at a 7% WACC, with revenue growing from $5.6B to $17.6B, and sensitivity analysis flagged pricing as the largest downside risk at $3.4B of NPV.

Excel, NPV and cash flow modeling, sensitivity analysis, SWOT, PowerPoint

Personal project

Oracle: Long

A long recommendation on Oracle at $147 with a $170 price target. Oracle's backlog of contracted revenue grew 46% to $664B while the stock fell 51%, and the pitch argues the market repriced a funded backlog as a debt-financed bet. Built a five-year DCF with an EBIT exit multiple, cross-checked against forward P/E and a sum-of-the-parts, with a sensitivity range of $111 to $218.

DCF, WACC, forward multiples, sum-of-the-parts, sensitivity analysis, Excel
